GroundingAnomalySpatially-Grounded Diffusion for Few-Shot Anomaly Synthesis

Yishen Liu et al.

Designed and deployed a Stable Diffusion-based few-shot framework to generate high-quality anomalous images with pixel-level masks, augmenting scarce industrial anomaly data and improving downstream anomaly detection.

Inside the research +

University-Industry Joint Project / Student Lead
Li Auto · May 2025 – Nov. 2025

Algorithm

Designed a Spatial Conditioning Module that fuses disentangled product and anomaly embeddings with a pixel-level semantic map to control anomaly location and class. Designed a gated self-attention module to inject these conditions into U-Net attention layers, enabling efficient few-shot adaptation of a frozen pretrained U-Net and pixel-level masked anomaly synthesis.

02CVPR 2027
Submitted to CVPR 2027 · Under review

FlowSteerInference-Time Alignment of Flow Matching Models with Learned Process Rewards

Yishen Liu et al.

Designed an inference-time alignment method based on a process reward model (PRM). With generator weights frozen, the method scores, guides, and resamples noisy latent states at arbitrary timesteps to improve human-preference scores.

Inside the research +

Internship Research / Project Lead
Meitu Inc · Nov. 2025 – May 2026

Process reward model

Proposed and trained a PRM on noisy latents, combining the Plackett-Luce loss for ranking with the Soft Bradley-Terry loss for calibrating reward-guidance gradients.

FlowSteer

Built an inference-time algorithm combining PRM gradient reward guidance with Feynman-Kac resampling. PRM gradients guide candidate-state generation, while PRM scores define the resampling potential for state screening, steering samples toward human preferences.
