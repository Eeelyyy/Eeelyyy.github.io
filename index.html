Project details

Industrial Anomaly Image Generation · Li Auto · May 2025 – Nov. 2025

Student Lead · University-Industry Joint Project

Designed and deployed a Stable Diffusion-based few-shot framework to generate high-quality anomalous images with pixel-level masks, augmenting scarce industrial anomaly data and improving downstream anomaly detection.

  • Algorithm. Designed a Spatial Conditioning Module that fuses disentangled product and anomaly embeddings with a pixel-level semantic map to control anomaly location and class. Designed a gated self-attention module to inject these conditions into U-Net attention layers, enabling efficient few-shot adaptation of a frozen pretrained U-Net and pixel-level masked anomaly synthesis.

Project details

FlowSteer: Inference-Time Alignment for Generative Models · Meitu Inc · Nov. 2025 – May 2026

Project Lead · Internship Research

Designed an inference-time alignment method based on a process reward model (PRM). With generator weights frozen, the method scores, guides, and resamples noisy latent states at arbitrary timesteps to improve human-preference scores.

  • Process reward model. Proposed and trained a PRM on noisy latents, combining the Plackett-Luce loss for ranking with the Soft Bradley-Terry loss for calibrating reward-guidance gradients.
  • FlowSteer. Built an inference-time algorithm combining PRM gradient reward guidance with Feynman-Kac resampling. PRM gradients guide candidate-state generation, while PRM scores define the resampling potential for state screening, steering samples toward human preferences.
